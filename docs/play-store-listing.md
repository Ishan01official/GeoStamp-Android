# Google Play listing — GPS Map Camera: GeoStamp

Prepared: 2026-10-11

## Recommended store name

**GPS Map Camera: GeoStamp**

Why this name: it keeps the GeoStamp brand while placing the strongest descriptive search terms — GPS, Map, Camera — directly in the title. Google Play limits app titles to 30 characters, and promotional claims such as “No Ads” should not be placed in the title or icon. The ad-free benefit is instead highlighted in the descriptions.

**GPS photo & video stamps with map, address & time. Private, free and ad-free.**

77 characters.

## Full description

GPS Map Camera: GeoStamp is a privacy-first camera for adding useful location details directly to photos and videos.

Capture a photo or video and save a clear stamp containing the date, time, GPS coordinates, address, map, compass direction, altitude and optional weather information.

### Built for real-world use

GeoStamp is useful for site inspections, construction, field work, delivery proof, property visits, travel, surveys, maintenance records and any situation where you want a photo or video to show where and when it was captured.

### Photo and video GPS stamps

- GPS coordinates
- Full available address
- Date and time
- Mini map
- GPS accuracy
- Compass direction
- Altitude
- Optional weather
- Custom text and logo
- Multiple stamp templates

### Photo, Video and Dual Capture

Use normal Photo and Video modes or compatible Dual Photo and Dual Video modes to capture the front and rear cameras together. Dual capture is shown only when the phone supports real concurrent cameras.

### Edit an address before capture

Reverse geocoding can occasionally return incomplete or slightly inaccurate address details. GeoStamp lets you correct the displayed address before capture without changing the actual recorded GPS coordinates.

### Private by design

GeoStamp has:

- No advertising
- No analytics SDK
- No tracking SDK
- No account requirement
- No automatic media uploads

Photos and videos are processed on your device. Network access is used only for enabled features such as address lookup, map tiles and optional weather data.

### Works without location too

Location permission is optional. The camera still works when location access is disabled; location-dependent stamp fields are simply omitted.

### Designed to stay out of the way

The camera interface is built around fast capture, clean controls and readable stamps instead of banners, pop-ups and advertising interruptions.

## Data safety / permissions checklist

Before submission, ensure Play Console declarations match the actual build:

- Camera: core app functionality.
- Precise/approximate location: optional foreground-only location stamping.
- Microphone: optional video audio; muted recording should work without audio permission where supported.
- Internet: address lookup, OpenStreetMap tiles and optional Open-Meteo weather requests.
- No advertising SDK.
- No analytics SDK.
- No account creation.
- No background location.

The public privacy policy must match the released binary, not only the source tree.
